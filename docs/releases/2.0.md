# 2.0: from beta to release candidate

[Overview](../../README.md) · [Current guides](../README.md) · [Version-by-version changes](../../CHANGELOG.md) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

The 2.0 cycle rebuilds the integration around isolated accounts, coordinated realtime updates and a durable ride archive. **107 betas, b0–b106**, lead to **v2.0.0rc1**. The timeline below covers every beta; current guides describe the resulting behavior, while individual releases preserve intermediate decisions.

## rc2: adaptive updates and lifecycle refinement

Following rc1, the second candidate separates polling targets from observation validity, runtime caches and the ten-minute ride-completion window. Charging and ACC signals adjust only their vehicle's groups; finite transition/stop windows settle current data without rescanning saved history. Five native profiles replace the numeric interval field, with explicit migration for existing custom settings.

Export cancellation now drains pending filesystem work before cleanup. Archive closure waits off-loop for its worker to finish. Recovered discovery publishes immediately, and explicit native refreshes complete due observations. Polling-only saves retain the session, archive and entity identities. CI combines affected scopes, pins HA boundaries centrally and keeps a documentation result available for every candidate.

## What the cycle delivers

### Account and entity lifecycle

- Separate sign-in sessions, configuration and historical archives for each account, with reauthentication and reconfiguration.
- Managed authenticated local communication, credential-safe setup and reviewed data normalization.
- Canonical account/vehicle-scoped entity IDs, migration journals, collision handling and preserved meaningful identities, user names and Recorder history.
- Main battery measurements, separate emergency battery SOC, three range fields, charging, ACC, service information, location and vehicle images. Battery specifications stay separate from measured health/capacity.
- English and Simplified Chinese guides, translated entity/settings/action labels and diagnostic Repairs for actionable problems.

### Realtime resilience and vehicle control

- Per-vehicle/group demand, freshness, shared in-flight reads, bounded concurrency, backoff and partial-failure isolation.
- Fast degraded-discovery recovery, independent status scheduling and immediate publication of successful updates; background history shares budgets without taking over live polling.
- Vehicle and seat Lock entities share the button executor. A command is sent once; accelerated readback confirms transitions within a 15-second window. New commands can replace an older observer.
- A verified seat unlock remains displayed for at least five seconds, with bounded latch follow-up. Latch feedback does not identify whether the lid was opened.

### Ride data and native Home Assistant features

- Typed ride/detail/track models preserve field provenance, timestamps and original precision. Maximum speed, overall average and unverified point samples remain distinct.
- Month/today/yesterday/latest summaries, weighted average energy consumption, local query Actions, a ride calendar and semantic vehicle/trip events.
- First-setup and date-boundary index repair works independently of optional full-detail synchronization. Known same-period historical facts remain usable during transient failures.
- Explicit import of verified closed periods supports native long-term statistics charts. Large ride lists, raw objects and GPS tracks stay outside entity attributes and Recorder.

### Incremental history and evidence protection

- Durable month/page discovery, ride-ID deduplication, incremental missing-detail acquisition, shared account budgets, restart checkpoints and fair multi-vehicle work.
- Independent completeness scopes for monthly lists, ride membership, saved details and track quality. Missing or repeated pages remain repairable gaps.
- Latest scalar facts and best geometry are retained separately, preventing later simplified tracks from replacing better saved routes.
- Private compressed originals, bounded audits, hash/reference checks, corruption isolation and evidence-backed reconstruction support local verification and recovery.

### One portable archive per account

- Schema 7 brings rides, details, source evidence and checkpoints into one private account-owned SQLite archive. Database work is serialized outside the event loop and updates use transactions.
- Account removal retains history by default. Re-adding the same authenticated account reconnects its archive and continues incremental work.
- Scoped account/vehicle exports and validated imports deduplicate records, preserve better geometry and merge matching evidence. Versioned ZIP manifests, SHA256 hashes and distinct filenames support migration.
- Verified legacy copies are consolidated and cleaned up; failed or unknown sources remain available for recovery. Capacity limits pause new writes, and saved records have no age-based expiry.

### Local browsing and distribution

- Authorized local pages, bounded track chunks, revision-aware cursors and local update notifications support efficient history access.
- The bundled map and linked cards provide an **early-alpha** exploration surface. Navigation, route comparison and interaction will continue to evolve independently of the archive.
- HACS installs a packaged integration with runtime assets inside its directory. Publication verifies the candidate, package/checksum and required checks before releasing a draft.
- Topic branches integrate into `dev`; accepted cycles promote to `main` with normal merges. Fixed-candidate milestone validation complements small, relevant checks during development.

## Decisions carried into RC

| Earlier approach | RC direction |
|---|---|
| SOC-derived energy/quality estimates | Retired; use verified reported values and optional nominal specifications. |
| Runtime-only manual history indexes | Persistent account-owned archive with explicit coverage and query responses. |
| Latest response replaces a saved route | Latest facts and best geometry selected independently. |
| Every ride becomes state or a large attribute | Compact current summaries, semantic events, response Actions and a local archive. |
| Engine commands or lock-latch feedback interpreted broadly | Maintainer-confirmed vehicle-lock mapping; seat latch kept distinct from lid position. |
| Experimental SMS sign-in | Hidden pending delivery verification; password-first flow. |
| History tied to entry deletion | Retained by default and reattached through verified account identity. |
| Full test matrix on every small change | Scoped checks plus a full pinned run at major boundaries. |

### 最终形成的能力

- **账号和实体生命周期：** 多账号独立会话／配置／历史库，重新认证与配置；安全的本机通信和字段规范化；账号／车辆范围实体 ID、迁移凭据及冲突处理，保留有意义身份、用户命名与 Recorder 历史。主电池、应急电池、三种续航、充电、ACC、服务、位置及图片分别表达；额定规格与实测能力分开。英中指南与多语言标签、可处理问题的 Repairs 一并维护。
- **实时与控制：** 逐车逐组需求、响应年龄、共享在途请求、有界并发、退避及部分失败隔离；发现降级快速恢复，独立状态调度，成功立即更新。锁和按钮共享执行器，指令发送一次，15 秒内加速确认，新指令可替换旧观察。座桶确认解锁显示至少 5 秒并有界跟随锁舌，锁舌状态不证明盖子位置。
- **行程与 HA 原生能力：** 明确行程／详情／轨迹模型、来源、时间及原始精度，区分最高速度、总平均速度和未知单位采样；本月／今日／昨日／最近摘要、加权平均能耗、本地 Action、日历及语义事件。首次配置与跨日索引修复独立于完整详情同步；短暂失败保留同统计期有效历史。显式导入已验证闭合周期以使用原生长期统计；大列表、原始对象和轨迹不进入实体属性或 Recorder。
- **增量与证据保护：** 持久月份／分页、ID 去重、缺失详情补取、共享额度、重启断点和多车公平调度；列表、月份关联、详情及轨迹质量分别计算覆盖。最新标量与较好轨迹分别保留；压缩原件、有界审计、摘要／引用校验、损坏隔离及有证据的重建支持长期恢复。
- **账号长期档案：** schema 7 将行程、详情、来源及断点整合进账号私有 SQLite，串行执行器在事件循环之外事务读写。删除账号默认保留，同账号重新登录接回；按账号／车辆导出、校验合并导入，去重并保留较好轨迹。版本化 ZIP、SHA256 及明确文件名支持迁移；已验证旧副本整合后清理，失败或未知来源保留，容量满暂停写入，已存记录无按时间自动过期。
- **本地浏览与分发：** 授权分页、轨迹分段、版本化游标、本地通知提高访问效率；地图和联动卡片为早期 alpha，独立演进。HACS 安装完整集成包，发布前核对候选、ZIP／摘要和必要检查。短分支进入 dev，验收后正常合入 main；小改相关验证，大阶段固定候选综合验证。

### RC 延续的决策

| 早期方式 | RC 方向 |
|---|---|
| SOC 推导能耗／质量估算 | 已退役，使用可信上报数据与可选额定参数。 |
| 仅运行时手动历史索引 | 账号长期档案、明确覆盖与查询响应。 |
| 新响应替换旧轨迹 | 最新标量与较好轨迹独立选择。 |
| 每条行程进入实体／大属性 | 小体积当前摘要、语义事件、Action 与本地档案。 |
| 宽泛解释发动机命令／锁舌 | 车辆锁使用维护者确认映射，座桶锁舌与盖子状态分开。 |
| 实验短信登录 | 等待投递验证，暂时隐藏，密码优先。 |
| 历史随条目删除 | 默认保留，按认证账号身份接回。 |
| 每个小改跑完整矩阵 | 按范围检查，大阶段跑固定版本综合验证。 |
